## Decision rule

Use the accounting backtest to set the center of the distribution. Use the thought leader to define causal upside/downside cases and disconfirming evidence. Promote a person into the numeric ensemble only after at least five comparable thesis families, three matured outcomes, 24 months of searched history, and metric-compatible forecasts exist.
