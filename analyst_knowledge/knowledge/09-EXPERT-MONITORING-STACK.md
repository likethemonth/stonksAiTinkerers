## Why the specialists are not silently ranked

- Todd Tomalak's public material is unusually relevant to Home Depot's end markets, but the captured archive did not yet yield enough matured, company-specific thesis families for an author score.
- Machinery Pete has decades of equipment-market data and made dated predictions about tractor/combine values and production restraint. Those are powerful Deere-cycle signals, but they are not Deere EPS forecasts and belong in an operational lane.
- Pawel Adrjan, Neil Carberry, and Tera Allas can improve the Hays demand model. Their industry views cannot be credited as Hays calls unless they explicitly map the mechanism to Hays before the outcome.
- SemiAnalysis, Serenity, and Keith Gill are useful archetypes or adjacent experts, but no qualifying target-company public history was found for these four companies. They remain excluded rather than assigned fake zeroes.
