# Wall Street Weather visual direction

## Locked north star

`preferred-aesthetic-reference.png` is the approved aesthetic reference: the first **Institutional Storm Terminal** concept.

Preserve in future concept generations:

- the elegant serif display typography;
- near-black graphite background;
- warm ivory text and restrained antique-gold accents;
- small ice-blue electrical highlights;
- spacious, institutional financial-terminal composition;
- fine rules, disciplined alignment, and minimal visual noise;
- a premium, serious atmosphere rather than a game or sci-fi interface.

## Detail target

Use the information depth of the later **Atmospheric Research Observatory** concept without inheriting its saturated blue palette, literal landscape, crowded cards, or smaller typography. Desired detail includes:

- evidence-source categories;
- audit and reasoning events;
- engine weights and estimates;
- rejected paths and scenarios;
- confidence, validation, and run progress;
- visible mapping from each reasoning event to a forecast revision.

## Animation contract

1. Begin tightly framed on the evidence cloud.
2. Charge and faintly disclose possible paths.
3. Strike downward with variable acceleration.
4. Pause at every real forecast revision.
5. Reveal concise reasoning and numerical impact.
6. Change horizontal trajectory and continue.
7. Let rejected branches extinguish before submission.
8. Ignite the final value on impact.
9. Zoom out to reveal the complete accepted and rejected trace.
10. Periodically re-illuminate the accepted path from top to bottom.

Do not treat implementation visuals as approved until the concept direction is explicitly confirmed.
